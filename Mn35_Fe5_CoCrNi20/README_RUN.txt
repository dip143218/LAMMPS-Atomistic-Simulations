Important scientific distinction
  LAMMPS directly gives relaxed energy, volume, structural density, phase-screen energies and quenched structures.
  Mean melting point, mean atomic radius, VEC, entropy, delta and Lambda are direct formula/property-table calculations.
  Exact web-app probability cannot be generated by LAMMPS.
